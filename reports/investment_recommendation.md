# ₹10 Crore Investment Recommendation

## Recommendation

Recommend investing the ₹10 crore in **better borrower targeting**.

The investment should not be deployed as an immediate full-scale rollout.
Instead, it should fund a controlled targeting experiment using borrower risk,
DPD, historical response, channel behaviour, calling time and attempt frequency.

## Why Targeting?

The analysis shows that overall recovery is approximately 10.87%, which is
consistent with the reported 11% recovery rate.

However, the claim of sustained 11% month-on-month improvement is not supported.
Recovery fluctuated across the observed months.

Contact rates across SMS, WhatsApp, Field and Voice are also very similar,
with SMS highest at 25.15%. Therefore, the evidence does not justify putting
the entire investment into a single communication channel.

Better targeting offers a broader opportunity to decide:

- who should be contacted,
- when they should be contacted,
- through which channel,
- how frequently they should be contacted,
- and when collection effort should be reduced or escalated.

## Proposed Experiment

Randomly divide eligible accounts into:

### Control
Continue the existing targeting strategy.

### Treatment
Use the improved targeting strategy based on:

- DPD
- risk segment
- historical payment behaviour
- PTP behaviour
- previous contact outcomes
- preferred/most responsive channel
- calling time
- attempt frequency

## Primary Success Metric

Incremental successful recovery per rupee of outstanding balance.

## Secondary Metrics

- Contact rate
- PTP rate
- KEPT PTP rate
- PTP-to-payment conversion
- Recovery per account
- Cost per ₹ recovered
- Complaint rate

## Decision Rule

Scale the treatment only if it produces a statistically and economically
meaningful improvement in recovery compared with the control group.

## ROI Limitation

The current dataset is observational and does not provide enough evidence to
honestly estimate the incremental recovery caused by a new targeting strategy.

Therefore, an exact ROI and break-even figure should not be fabricated.

The proposed experiment should first estimate incremental recovery and establish
the confidence interval required for the final investment decision.

## Key Evidence

- Successful recovery: ₹131.56 crore
- Reconstructed recovery rate: 10.87%
- Reported recovery rate: 11%
- Payment success rate: 70.14%
- KEPT PTPs: 4,489
- KEPT PTPs followed by successful payment: 928
- PTP-to-successful-payment rate: 20.67%
- Highest observed targeting contact rate: SMS at 25.15%

## Risks

The analysis is subject to selection bias, attribution-window limitations and
other observational-data limitations.

The treatment/control experiment is therefore required before claiming causal
incremental recovery.
